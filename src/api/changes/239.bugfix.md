Fix the angular distance from the FOV center reported for ephemeris-refined satellite positions. It was computed with a flat-sky `sqrt((ra1 - ra2)**2 + (dec1 - dec2)**2)`, which ignores cos(dec) foreshortening in right ascension and breaks across the RA 0/360 degree boundary. It now uses a haversine great-circle separation, so positions at high declination and near RA = 0 are placed in or out of the field of view correctly.
